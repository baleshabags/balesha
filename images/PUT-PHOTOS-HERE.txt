Put your photos in this folder. The website finds them by their file names.

PRODUCT PHOTOS (the swipe slider on each bag)
Name them with the product id, then -1, -2, -3 and so on:

  rani-top-handle-1.jpg   rani-top-handle-2.jpg   rani-top-handle-3.jpg
  gulmohar-tote-1.jpg     ...
  shaadi-potli-1.jpg      ...
  noor-clutch-1.jpg       ...
  chandni-sling-1.jpg     ...
  mehr-wallet-1.jpg       ...
  riwaaz-satchel-1.jpg    ...
  kantha-shopper-1.jpg    ...

Up to 8 photos per bag. Numbers must start at 1 with no gaps
(if -2 is missing, -3 and later won't be shown).

OTHER PHOTOS (optional)
  hero.jpg        the big bag photo at the top of the page
  featured.jpg    the featured-piece photo (otherwise noor-clutch-1 is used)
  insta-1.jpg to insta-4.jpg   the four Instagram tiles
  founder.jpg     your photo in the founder card (otherwise your initials show)

TIPS
- .jpg, .jpeg, .png and .webp all work.
- Use lowercase names exactly as above, with no spaces.
- Portrait photos (3:4, for example 1200 x 1600 pixels) look best.
- Keep each photo under about 500 KB so the site loads fast on phones.
- Any bag without photos keeps its drawing, so you can add photos one bag at a time.
